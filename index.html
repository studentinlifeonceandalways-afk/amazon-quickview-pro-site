Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Amazon Quick View Pro respects your privacy. This extension does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information. All research profiles, product bookmarks, and settings are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's sync storage. No data is transmitted to external servers except for optional exchange rate fetching (via Cloudflare Worker) and bulk ASIN checks performed through our GDPR-compliant proxy worker which does NOT log ASINs permanently. We do not track browsing history or share any data with third parties.

Data usage: The extension accesses Amazon product pages only when you actively browse them. No analytics, no cookies.

By using this extension, you agree to the local storage of your research profiles.

Data Deletion: All data can be removed by uninstalling the extension or clearing Chrome's storage.
